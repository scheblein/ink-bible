# Controls

Open **Settings → Controls** to configure buttons and touch input. The available
options depend on the device.

- **Left / Right buttons** (non-touch devices): sets front Left / Right to
  `Chapter / Book` (default: short press skips chapters, long press skips books),
  `Page turn`, or `Links and Footnotes`. Automatically falls back to page turns
  if side buttons are disabled.
- **Front button follow orientation** & **Remap front buttons** (non-touch
  devices): rotate or remap front button assignments.
- **Side button layout** & **Long-press behavior**: choose page-turn direction
  and whether holding a page-turn button skips chapters or rotates orientation.
- **Short power button**: assign short-press Power behavior (including footnote
  return when set to `Footnotes`).
- **Double-click power frontlight** (X4 Pro): toggle the frontlight with a
  double click of the power button.
- **Home button** (devices with a Home key, such as X4 Pro): configure Tap,
  Double Tap, and Long Press actions.
- **Touch controls** (touch devices): configure reading tap/swipe page turns and
  menu gestures.
